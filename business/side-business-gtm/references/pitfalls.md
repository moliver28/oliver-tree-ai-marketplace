## Pitfalls
- First-touch emails that lead with credentials flop; lead with the buyer's
  budget moment (the decision they are about to make) and the fixed-fee
  framing that beats hourly expert rates.
- A free first deliverable is the trust accelerator when there is zero social
  proof; hold it back only when the buyer already knows the analyst.
- Advisory-content businesses (compliance guides, authentication) carry
  liability and product-rot the others do not; flag both before
  recommending.
- Platform-dependent plays (search ranking, marketplace rules) are acceptable
  only when the plan diversifies across sites or niches; state the dependency
  in the ranking, never bury it.

## Scheduling
- Reminders for outreach waves: schedule one-shot reminder jobs per wave on
  the operator's own automation or scheduler facility, delivered to their
  preferred notification channel (verified chat handle or inbox). Reminders
  point at the wave; they never auto-send the emails, and the operator sends
  personally.
- On chat platforms with thread-targeted delivery, an unfilled thread
  reference lands the message in the main channel instead of the thread;
  check the thread field before scheduling.
- If reminders @-mention anyone, use the operator's own verified handle or ID
  as configured per installation; never a guessed account.
