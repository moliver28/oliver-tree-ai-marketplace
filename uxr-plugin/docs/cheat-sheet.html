Interview phases (new)

The new interview moves through these phases; every listens for edits and never creates without a preview.

  1. Scope - one message, three questions: working title, group or area, decision owner.
  2. Evidence sweep - every source in one parallel batch; failed sources log one skipped line.
  3. Shareability - each Drive link tagged shared, private, or unknown; permissions never change.
  4. Background - a 3 to 5 sentence draft, edited before moving on.
  5. Key decision - one sentence that names what changes and who owns it.
  6. Hypotheses and assumptions - 3 to 5 falsifiable hypotheses, 2 to 3 assumptions, as Label. Claim.
  7. Research questions - 3 to 6 open questions with the RQ-to-hypothesis map; the HCD reviewer checks the set.
  8. Method, fields, create - the method table row, the field panel, a preview, then create and init on your go.

Role cards

hcd-researcher

Human-centered design review of drafts and studies; strengths, risks, and changes with confidence-rated findings.

stakeholder-reviewer

Reads a report as one role (PD, EM, PM, Leadership) and returns 3 to 7 cited bullets.

novice-report-reader

First-time reader of the rendered report; flags orientation, terms, and dead ends.

research-dataviz-designer

Reviews charts and HTML rendering: chart type, contrast, disclosure structure.

expert-participant

Synthetic expert user for script dry runs; flags over-explanation and redundant questions.

novice-participant

Synthetic first-time user; flags unfamiliar terms and ambiguous instructions.

script-writer

Applies the reviewers' minimal fixes to the interview script without changing structure.

FAQ

Where does my data live?

Study specs live in <state_dir>/specs/, session journals in <state_dir>, and per-study files in the workspace folder init creates. Participant keys and raw transcripts are gitignored and never committed.

Why does everything arrive as a draft?

Comments, chat messages, mail, decks, and publishes wait for your explicit go. The plugin computes; you decide.

What is the spec file?

The JSON source of truth for a study ticket (<state_dir>/specs/<KEY>.spec.json). Edits patch it, validate it, and push the rendered body in one write.

How is a deck produced?

From an outline JSON by the deck generator with a geometry lint. The .pptx is always regenerated, never hand-edited, and holds no raster images.

What broke the cheat sheet?

Nothing should: uxr-cheatsheet re-syncs this page from its sources on demand and never rewrites the editorial sections.
