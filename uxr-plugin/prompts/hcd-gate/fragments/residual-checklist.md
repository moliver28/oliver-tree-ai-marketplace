Judge only this checklist, item by item:

- claim-evidence trace: every finding's claim rests on a named source, and
  a claim resting on outside evidence carries 2 authoritative sources or
  the exact source "insufficient external evidence".
- method/RQ alignment: the questions match what the method can answer
  (a comparative "vs" framing is out of scope for an interview, and a
  decision restated as a hypothesis cannot be resolved by sessions).
- stance justification: every verdict or stance line says why in one line,
  and unanswered items keep their exact "no evidence" wording instead of
  invented support.
- participant respect: no leading wording; the study only asks what
  participants can actually answer.
