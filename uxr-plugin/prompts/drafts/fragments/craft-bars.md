Quality bars the drafts must meet:

- A hypothesis is falsifiable, single-mechanism, and tied to the decision:
  push back with a rewrite on claims that are not falsifiable, mix two
  mechanisms, or change nothing about the decision when false.
- An assumption is something the team treats as true today, checkable in
  the same sessions.
- Good decision shapes: "Should X ship to GA, and under what conditions?",
  "Do we invest in A or B for the next release?", "Which of these flows do
  we standardize on?" Bad shapes: a topic ("Understand how users feel"),
  presupposed answers ("Validate X"), two sentences, or no owner.
