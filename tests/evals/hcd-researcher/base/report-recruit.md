# Report: new user navigation study

## Method

5 moderated sessions with first-week users, 30 minutes each, think-aloud.
Transcripts filed under interviews/; quotes below are speaker-labeled and
verbatim.

## Key Findings

- Participants who found the timeline started a plan in the same session [S:s1]
- Participants asked for the timeline in the place they already look [S:s2]

## Implications

First-session planning was observed in every session where the timeline
was found, so the entry point placement carries the outcome.

- Share the report with the support team leads
- Decide the entry point at the January planning review with this evidence
