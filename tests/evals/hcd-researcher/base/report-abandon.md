# Report: new user navigation study

## Method

5 moderated sessions with first-week users, 30 minutes each, think-aloud.
Transcripts filed under interviews/; quotes below are speaker-labeled and
verbatim.

## Key Findings

- 5 of 5 participants found the timeline within two minutes [S:s1]
- Participants looked in the header toolbar first, before the left
  navigation [S:s1, S:s2]

## Implications

The header toolbar is where first-week users look for planning features;
the left navigation change would not meet them where they look.
